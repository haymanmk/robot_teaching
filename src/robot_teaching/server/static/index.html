Safety notes

    
    
      
  • Connect enables the motors and holds the pose; Disconnect and Ctrl+C move the arm home, then switch the motors off.
    • 
      
  • Playback starts from hold mode only, from where the arm is.
    • 
      
  • Stop motion (button, Space or Esc) ramps the speed down and holds; E-STOP cuts motor torque — a loaded arm will fall.
    • 
      
  • Keyboard stops only work while this page has focus; keep the hardware e-stop within reach.
    • 
      
  • Start free drive from a clearance pose; contact with the table corrupts the gravity model.
    •

Pick a joint to watch it follow its setpoint. A sawtooth in the error at low speed is stick-slip; gaps shared by every joint point at the bus; a 3–4 Hz ring on one joint is low damping. The commanded torque is kp·(error) + kd·(velocity error) + feed-forward; the reported torque comes from the motor's feedback frame.
