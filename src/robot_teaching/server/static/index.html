Safety notes

    
    
      
  • Connect enables the motors and holds the pose; Disconnect and Ctrl+C move the arm home, then switch the motors off.
    • 
      
  • Playback starts from hold mode only, from where the arm is.
    • 
      
  • Stop motion (button, Space or Esc) ramps the speed down and holds; E-STOP cuts motor torque — a loaded arm will fall.
    • 
      
  • Keyboard stops only work while this page has focus; keep the hardware e-stop within reach.
    • 
      
  • Start free drive from a clearance pose; contact with the table corrupts the gravity model.
    • 
      
  • Reload gains (Scope card) re-reads the hold, free-drive and gripper kp/kd, tau_scale and the friction feed-forward from the YAML files and fades them in; no restart. Limits and the home position still need one.

Pick a joint to watch it follow its setpoint. A sawtooth in the error at low speed is stick-slip; gaps shared by every joint point at the bus; a 3–4 Hz ring on one joint is low damping. The commanded torque is kp·(error) + kd·(velocity error) + feed-forward; the feed-forward trace is the gravity, friction and inertia terms switched on in the Playback card, and the recording keeps the three apart; the reported torque comes from the motor's feedback frame. −/+ zoom the window between 0.5 s and 60 s. Pause freezes the traces (drag one to look back in time; the axis then shows the controller clock, the t_s of a recording). Record writes every sample of the joint to a CSV file that downloads on Stop. Spectrum adds the amplitude spectrum of one signal over the samples on screen, highest peak above 1 Hz marked: a wobble is a peak in the error; the same peak in the commanded torque means the loop is driving it, a peak only in the measured position or the reported torque points at the mechanics. max |error| is the largest tracking error over every sample received since the joint was picked or Reset max was pressed, not only over the samples on screen; a hole in a trace means samples were missing.
