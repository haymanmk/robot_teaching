The same strips as the pendant's live scope, for a CSV written by its Record button: every sample of one joint. Drag a trace to move through time, Ctrl+wheel or −/+ to zoom (keys: ← → move by half a window, − + zoom), Fit shows the whole run; hovering a trace reads the sample under the pointer. A hole in a trace means samples were missing. A recording that spans a server restart has more than one run (the clock starts again from zero); each is listed on its own. Spectrum adds the amplitude spectrum of one signal over the samples on screen, highest peak above 1 Hz marked: a wobble is a peak in the error; the same peak in the commanded torque means the loop is driving it, a peak only in the measured position or the reported torque points at the mechanics.
