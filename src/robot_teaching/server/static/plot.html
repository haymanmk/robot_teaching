The same strips as the pendant's live scope, for a CSV written by its Record button: every sample of one joint. Drag a trace to move through time, Ctrl+wheel or −/+ to zoom (keys: ← → move by half a window, − + zoom), Fit shows the whole run; hovering a trace reads the sample under the pointer. Untick a trace in a legend to hide it for the moment; the scale then fits the traces left. Each strip fits its value axis to the samples on screen until you zoom it: −/+/Auto at the right of its title or Shift+wheel over the trace, after which a drag also moves it up and down; a zoomed strip also grows taller, up to three times, so zooming in buys pixels as well. The tracking-error strip shows the position error or the torque error (reported by the motor − commanded). The torque strip also shows the feed-forward the command contains, and the spectrum offers its gravity, friction and inertia terms one by one, to tell a wobble the feed-forward drives from one the feedback drives. Compare overlays a second recording, B, dashed on the same axes with its start on the start of A; offset or Shift+drag on a trace slides B in time, and the readouts, the hover values and the spectrum then show A and B together. A hole in a trace means samples were missing. A recording that spans a server restart has more than one run (the clock starts again from zero); each is listed on its own. Spectrum adds the amplitude spectrum of one signal over the samples on screen, highest peak above 1 Hz marked: a wobble is a peak in the error; the same peak in the commanded torque means the loop is driving it, a peak only in the measured position or the reported torque points at the mechanics.
